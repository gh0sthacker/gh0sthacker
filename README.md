## `> Responsible Disclosure Process`

For vulnerabilities in open source software, I follow this process:

1. **Report through the project's documented security channel** — `/security`, `SECURITY.md`, email, GHSA, private portal, VDP, or VRP.
2. **If no private channel exists, or after 30 days without response** to an email or GHSA submission, I may open a public issue **without a PoC**.
3. **If acknowledged**, I send up to two follow-up messages over the following weeks to check remediation progress.
4. **Where maintainers request an extension**, target a disclosure timeline of up to **90 days** for remediation and coordinated disclosure.
5. **If communication lapses for more than 30 days** after the last substantive response, or once the vuln is fixed, I may disclose through a **CNA**, where appropriate.

<details>
<summary><code>TL;DR timeline</code></summary>

| Stage | Window |
|-------|--------|
| Initial private report | Day 0 |
| Awaiting acknowledgment | 30 days |
| Follow-up nudges | up to 2, spaced weeks apart |
| Remediation window | up to 90 days (with extension) |
| Public / CNA disclosure | after fix or 30-day silence |

</details>

<details>
<summary><code>Out of scope</code></summary>

- DoS / volumetric attacks without research value
- Self-XSS, clickjacking on non-sensitive pages
- Reports requiring privileged access the reporter already has
- Missing security headers / banners without demonstrable impact
- Theoretical issues without a working PoC

</details>
